Files Concordance's book identity joined as one book. What they share depends on how they're joined:

  • Same text: the same book ID (a UUID in the EPUB) and the same body text. They share everything: highlights and notes, handmade TOC and flows, reading position (through KOReader's progress sync), and everything below.
  • Same book ID: another edition, or a file whose text no device has read yet. They share status, rating, review and custom metadata, and reading statistics.
  • Same title and authors: no device has read the file (gone before book identity read it, or without a book ID, such as a PDF). Reading statistics only.
